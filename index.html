how it works

Counts run locally in your browser — nothing leaves this page unless you share. Words split on whitespace; sentences end at . ! ? …; paragraphs on blank lines. Reading time assumes 200 wpm; speaking 130 wpm; pages ~250 words.

Voice dictation uses your browser’s built-in speech recognition (Web Speech API). Audio is handled by the browser or your device — not sent to a server for transcription.
